About Ink Library

Ink Library is a personal fountain pen ink library designed to make it easier to keep track of inks and their properties.

Features

  • Browse and filter the ink database by colour, effects and shading.
  • Keep track of inks in your personal library.
  • Manage your Wishlist and Owned inks.
  • Keep track of Currently Inked pens.
  • Save your library locally in the browser.

Future Development

The current version of Ink Library is a frontend project, but the idea could be expanded with a backend and user accounts. This could make it possible to store libraries online and eventually add features such as sharing collections and community interactions.
